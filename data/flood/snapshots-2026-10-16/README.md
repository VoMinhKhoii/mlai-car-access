The five-day bulletin 289/2026, issued 09:08 +07:00, is the numerical source for Phú An highs and lows on 16–20 October and observations on 15 October. The 09:00 bulletin 138-SG corroborates the falling tide trend; it does not provide the numerical five-day table.

No harmonic fallback was fitted. No values from the coastal Vũng Tàu station were used. The final published extremum is 18:00 on 20 October; later times remain unavailable. The missing ct high on 18 October is omitted, not invented. Cosine interpolation between the published extrema is an application approximation, not an official continuous time series.
