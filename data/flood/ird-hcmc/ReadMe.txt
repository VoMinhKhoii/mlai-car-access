*****Dataset citation*****


Hardy, Sebastien; Radziute, Gintaré, 2026, "Replication data for : Historical database of urban flooding in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam (2002–2026)", https://doi.org/10.23708/8Y16HU, DataSuds

*****Dataset description*****

 	
This dataset compiles 425 geolocated observations of urban flooding in Ho Chi Minh City, recorded between 2002 and 2026 from 53 sources (mainly Vietnamese newspapers, along with a few technical reports).
Each observation documents the date, location (name, type, ward/commune, and the current administrative unit following the 2025 administrative reform), water height (minimum/maximum in cm and depth class), cause (rain, high tide, or combined), impact type, source, spatial precision, and geocoding method.

The dataset also includes complementary layers:

    administrative boundaries before/after the 2025 reform
    population density by district (2019)
    flood recurrence by street
    rivers, canals and water bodies
    verified industrial zones

This work was carried out as part of a Master's (M2) internship (IGE, April–August 2026) within LMI CARE, under the supervision of Sébastien Hardy (IRD). The Master's thesis was defended on 10 September 2026.

Summary of methodology: multilingual documentary research (French, English, Vietnamese, and to a lesser extent Russian) using targeted keywords; manual extraction and geolocation of reported events; harmonization with administrative references before and after the 2025 reform (see https://bando.tphcm.gov.vn/); GIS processing under ArcGIS Pro.
