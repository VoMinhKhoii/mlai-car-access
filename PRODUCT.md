# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Ngõ Ngách serves private and ride-hailing drivers in Ho Chi Minh City. The field kit serves teammates measuring alleys on a phone outdoors, often with intermittent connectivity.

## Product Purpose

Give a vehicle-specific Arrival Plan grounded in sourced Clear Width evidence. Enable teammates to replace uncertain ranges with dated physical measurements, without confusing a single sample with a complete alley survey.

## Brand Personality

Practical, candid, local. Vietnamese task copy and explicit evidence provenance.

## Anti-references

Decorative dashboards, promotional hero sections, unexplained confidence scores, and interfaces that imply missing evidence means safe access.

## Design Principles

- Evidence and its limitations remain visible.
- Make the next useful action obvious.
- Keep controls readable and usable with one hand outdoors.
- Preserve work across network failures and explain unsaved work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Large touch targets, visible keyboard focus, native controls, readable contrast, Vietnamese labels, and reduced-motion support. No color-only status indicators.
