Will your car fit, and get back out?

Before a driver turns into a hẻm, we tell them, for their exact car, with the source behind every width.

The problem

Maps call every hẻm a road.

  1. 1The route says turn in.
  2. 2The alley narrows, or floods.
  3. 3The driver reverses out, or gets stuck.

No public map in Vietnam stores how wide an alley is.

The answer · Toyota Fortuner, 2.11 m to the mirrors

Same street. Two hẻm. Two answers.

Usable width is the gap between city land parcels minus 1 m for steps, stalls and parked bikes. Drawn to scale.

How we know

Five kinds of proof. The strongest decides.

Every width carries its source and date. A guess never overrules a measurement, and when we only have a guess, we say so.

  1. MeasuredTape or phone, on the groundfield kit
  2. OfficialGap between city cadastral parcels261
  3. ObservedDated photo, against a known object12,975
  4. DeclaredWhat a listing says: "hẻm xe hơi"1,781
  5. InferredGeometry estimate, shown as a range4

Over the whole stay

Getting in is half of it.

  • The way outThrough, a turn, or a reverse. Over 30 m backwards and the exit fails.
