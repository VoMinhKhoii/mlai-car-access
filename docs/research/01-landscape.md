## 1. Problem evidence

### Cars routed into alleys
- Thanh Niên, 2026-03-05: Google Maps leads drivers into hẻm "chỉ vừa thân xe"; routing ignores width and vehicle limits; Google's narrow-road avoidance has no Vietnam launch date [reported] https://thanhnien.vn/diem-tru-cua-google-maps-ung-dung-tren-o-to-185260304201330812.htm
- Đà Lạt, 2026-08-30: an SUV following Google Maps through Hẻm 41 Hoàng Hoa Thám spent 1h42m on narrow cemetery roads before it could turn around [reported] https://vietnamnet.vn/di-theo-google-maps-ca-gia-dinh-lac-sau-trong-nghia-trang-da-lat-giua-dem-toi-2551671.html
- Ride-hailing, VOZ thread 2025-11-08 (45 replies): about 5 of 10 GrabCar drivers stopped at the alley mouth and cancelled, although the alley fits two cars and has a turnaround. Reasons given: drivers don't know the layout, fear getting stuck or scratched, and the fare is too low [reported] https://voz.vn/t/grab-car-khong-chiu-vo-hem-du-hem-rong-du-2-chiec-xe-hoi-chay.1168405/

### Scale of alley access
- Khương Hạ fire, 2023-09: 56 dead; 15 fire trucks stopped about 400 m away because the alley is about 3 m wide [reported] https://vnexpress.net/vi-sao-nhieu-nguoi-chet-trong-vu-chay-chung-cu-mini-4653574.html
- Hanoi Decision 2613/QĐ-UBND (2026-05-22): 7,955 alleys longer than 200 m that fire trucks cannot reach [reported] https://daidoanket.vn/dua-nuoc-vao-ngo-sau-hoa-giai-nguy-co-hoa-hoan-o-thu-do.html
- HCMC Decision 4582/QĐ-UBND (2026-07-24): annex listing, street by street, roads and alleys fire trucks cannot enter across 74 wards and communes (e.g., Tân Đông Hiệp 686, Tân Phú 590) [documented] https://luatvietnam.vn/dat-dai/quyet-dinh-4582-qd-ubnd-tp-hcm-2026-cong-bo-danh-sach-khu-vuc-khong-dam-bao-ha-tang-giao-thong-nguon-nuoc-chua-chay-442277-d2.html
- 2016: 85% of HCMC residents and 88% of Hanoi residents lived in hẻm [reported, secondary] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%E1%BA%BBm
- OSM Vietnam: 3,136,788 highway ways; at most 2,772 carry `width` (≤0.09%) and 565 carry `maxwidth` [documented] https://taginfo.geofabrik.de/asia:vietnam/keys/width

## 3. Implications for the pitch (agent's notes)
- **Open space:** no product in Vietnam answers, for a specific car: can it get in, turn around and get out; how deep is the flood right now; where to park and how far to walk. Google's India launch shows demand and a working method; no Vietnam date yet.
- **Training labels exist:** the Hanoi 2613 and HCMC 4582 annexes, plus the few OSM width tags, could serve as ground truth.
- **Flood ties into Tasco's business:** insurers exclude drivers who chose to drive into water. Tasco Insurance wrote VND 1,564bn in premiums in 2025 and is top 7 in motor; VETC runs a 24/7 rescue service [reported] https://nhandan.vn/tasco-hoan-thien-nen-tang-tao-da-cho-tang-truong-nhanh-trong-nam-2026-post957345.html
- **Parking ties into VETC:** from 2026-07-01, about 3,000 Hanoi parking points must take electronic non-stop payment and share data with the authorities; VETC is one provider [reported] https://vnexpress.net/toan-bo-bai-giu-oto-tai-ha-noi-thu-phi-khong-dung-tu-thang-7-5092848.html. VETC has about 4.3M app users and about 75% of the electronic toll market [reported] https://vietnamnet.vn/vetc-thu-phi-duy-tri-vi-dien-tu-gan-80-000-dong-nam-tasco-huong-loi-ra-sao-2546627.html
- **The property market prices car access:** in Hanoi, homes on alleys where two cars can pass sell for more than VND 300M/m², against 150–180M on motorbike-only alleys [reported] https://vtv.vn/kinh-te/nha-trong-ngo-o-to-tranh-nhau-het-gia-300-trieu-dong-m2-20241209111023733.htm
- **Vehicle-specific routing is unclaimed:** no routing API in Vietnam takes car width, turning radius or ground clearance.
- **Don't overclaim:** Google already shows flood reports and parking on arrival worldwide. Our edge is a vehicle-specific answer plus Vietnamese alley geometry.
